The Landing Table

In Shadowdark Enhancer for Foundry VTT, the landing page is a table the party sits at between adventures. The things on it are the buttons: the GM presses one button to bring the whole table here, and one to send everyone back to the adventure.

Move over the table and click anything: buy from the chest, tick a quest, play the dice, open a portrait. Write on the title card.

One page, no setup

The module makes the table the first time the GM presses the Landing page button: the scene, a market stocked for the settlement the party is in, and the page. Nothing to upload or wire.

A market by settlement size

Every ware is rolled against its rarity for a Village, Town, City or City-State. Common goods are always there; rare ones turn up now and then. Each restock rolls the shelf again. Players shop with their own characters, even with no GM connected.

What is on the table

  • Quest tome: the active quests with tick boxes
  • Coin chest: the party's coin, opens to the market
  • Dice cup: ten games of chance
  • Goblets: carousing
  • Framed map: your party's explored hexes
  • Hourglass, journal, recap scrolls, letter, day dial
